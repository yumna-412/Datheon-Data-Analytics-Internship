# Task 1 – Superstore Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Superstore retail sales data to identify business trends, understand profitability, and evaluate sales performance across different product categories, regions, and customer segments.

The analysis was performed using Python and data visualization libraries.

## Objectives
- Clean and preprocess the retail sales dataset.
- Analyze sales and profit across product categories.
- Compare regional and customer segment performance.
- Examine monthly sales trends.
- Study the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Identify useful business insights and recommendations.

## Dataset
**Dataset:** Superstore Retail Sales

**Files:**
- `superstore_sales.csv` – Original dataset.
- `cleaned_superstore_sales.csv` – Cleaned dataset after preprocessing.

The dataset includes order details, customer information, product categories, sales, quantity, discounts, and profit.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook
- Visual Studio Code

## Project Workflow
1. Imported the required Python libraries.
2. Loaded and explored the dataset.
3. Checked missing values and duplicate records.
4. Cleaned incomplete records and processed date columns.
5. Created Year, Month, and Year-Month features.
6. Analyzed sales and profit by category, region, and segment.
7. Visualized monthly sales trends.
8. Examined discount and profit relationships.
9. Created charts and summary statistics.
10. Documented business findings and recommendations.

## Key Business Findings
- Technology was the most profitable product category.
- The West region recorded the highest sales and profit.
- The Consumer segment contributed the highest sales and profit.
- Higher discount levels were associated with negative average profit in several discount groups.
- November 2018 recorded the highest monthly sales in the analyzed period.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus on profitable product categories.
- Strengthen sales strategies in high-performing regions.
- Improve customer engagement in the Consumer segment.
- Review high-discount strategies to protect profit margins.
- Use monthly sales trends for inventory and marketing planning.
